1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to deliver our digital invitation services.

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Billing details
  • Event names and dates

  • Guest-facing content

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and customize your digital wedding invitation

  • Process your order and payment confirmation
  • Provide support and revisions
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention records

3. Payment Security

Payments may be processed using third-party payment gateways such as Razorpay or other approved providers.

Dvites does not store your:

  • Card number
  • CVV
  • UPI PIN
  • Net banking password
  • Sensitive payment credentials

All payment processing is handled by secure third-party payment providers.

4. Data Sharing

We only share limited information with trusted service providers when required for:

  • Payment processing
  • Website hosting
  • Analytics
  • Email delivery
  • Customer support
  • Legal or regulatory compliance

5. Data Retention

We keep your order and event data only for as long as needed to deliver your invitation, provide support, maintain records, or comply with legal obligations.

Your live invitation link may remain active until the agreed event timeline or wedding date, after which access may expire.

6. Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for ensuring that any photos, music, text, or personal content shared with us is lawful and does not violate any third-party rights.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Dvites after changes means you accept the updated policy.
